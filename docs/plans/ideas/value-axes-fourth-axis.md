# Value axes — the 4th-axis decision

Companion to `creative-core-exploration.md`, but built **from scratch off the
environmental-values literature** rather than the original straw-men. This doc holds the
research-grounded axis set and the open reflection on which **4th axis** to add.

> Status: exploration. Axes 1–3 are the working basis (high mutual independence, each
> backed by multiple validated frameworks). The 4th axis is **undecided** — this doc
> compares the candidates.

---

## What the literature forced on us (why we restarted)

The most rigorously factor-analysed instrument (Milfont & Duckitt's
[Environmental Attitudes Inventory](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0272494409000565))
collapses **12** attitude facets into just **2** higher-order factors (Preservation /
Utilization); the [NEP scale](https://spssi.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-4537.00176)
is essentially **one** general "ecological worldview" dimension. Lesson: environmental
ideology is **low-dimensional (~2.5–3 robust axes)**. Picking 5 facets of "how green are
you" would collapse the avatar space onto a diagonal and make archetypes indistinguishable —
*especially* for our users, who self-selected into an ecology app and are already green.

So: spend axes on **what *kind* of green** (orientation → archetype + surface), and let
**how *much* green** (intensity/urgency) live on the **XP / engagement clock** (this is the
[Six Americas](https://climatecommunication.yale.edu/about/projects/global-warmings-six-americas/)
gradient — Alarmed→Dismissive — which is a single engagement dimension, *not* a values space).

## The 3 locked axes (working basis)

Each maps to a **non-overlapping visual channel** — the test that the set is well-separated:
a Rive designer can move any one slider without visually contradicting the others.

| # | Axis | The question | Visual channel | Key grounding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Anthropocentric ↔ Ecocentric** | *Whose interests have moral standing?* nature-for-people ↔ nature has intrinsic worth | **Silhouette / kingdom** — humanoid/built ↔ wild/animal/plant | [O'Riordan](https://evs.institute/environment-and-society/ecocentric-vs-technocentric-ideologies/); [Steg & De Groot biospheric vs altruistic](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0013916506297831); [Thompson & Barton](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0272494405801689) |
| 2 | **Technological ↔ Sufficiency** | *How do we reconcile prosperity with the planet?* innovate & decouple ↔ consume less / limits | **Material / texture** — sleek/metallic ↔ earthen/woven/raw | [Ecomodernism vs degrowth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecomodernism); [bright vs dark green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bright_green_environmentalism) |
| 3 | **Individual ↔ Collective** *(group)* | *Where does change come from?* personal choices/markets ↔ policy/structural | **Multiplicity** — lone creature ↔ flock/colony/network | [Maniates](https://www.sindark.com/phd/thesis/sources/Maniates.pdf); [Stern's distinct behavior spheres](https://humanecologyreview.org/pastissues/her62/62sternetal.pdf); [near-zero private→collective spillover](https://climatecommunication.yale.edu/publications/does-personal-climate-change-mitigation-behavior-influence-collective-behavior-experimental-evidence-of-no-spillover-in-the-united-states/) |

## The 4th-axis candidates

### Candidate A — Technocratic ↔ Grassroots *(the "grid" dimension)*
*Who should be in charge of solving it?* Trust experts/institutions/scale (carbon markets,
IPCC-led policy, mega-projects, professional management) ↔ trust communities/distributed power
(energy co-ops, indigenous stewardship, commons, citizens' assemblies).

- **Source:** [Kahan's cultural cognition](https://www.culturalcognition.net/) hierarchy–egalitarianism;
  Cultural Theory's grid axis.
- **Orthogonality to 1–3:** **HIGH.** Kahan validates grid as roughly ⊥ to group (axis 3).
  Adds genuinely new variance.
- **Bonus:** Axes 3 × 4 (group × grid) are exactly the plane that generates Cultural Theory's
  four ["myths of nature"](https://www.rug.nl/staff/e.m.steg/stegandsieversmythsofnature.pdf)
  → validated archetype anchors for free.
- **Visual channel:** **Structure / form-order** — symmetric/architected/crystalline ↔
  asymmetric/organic-cluster/rhizomatic.
- **Caveat:** cultural cognition [replicates only weakly across 23 countries](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0272494422001104)
  — weaker signal in non-Anglo markets.

### Candidate B — Reformist ↔ Radical / "Urgency"
*How far, and how fast?* Work within institutions, incremental ↔ remake the system, urgent.

- **Source:** [Dryzek's master axis](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/politics-of-the-earth-9780198851745)
  (reformist↔radical); the urgency reading is the Six Americas engagement gradient.
- **The disambiguation that matters:** this merges two things — *scope of change sought*
  (reformist↔radical) and *temporal alarm* (urgency). **Both are magnitude/intensity
  constructs, not orientation constructs.**
- **Orthogonality to 1–3:** **LOW.** ~50% collinear with axis 2 (Means) — Dryzek's radical
  discourses (green radicalism, survivalism) bundle with limits/sufficiency; reformist ones
  (problem-solving, ecological modernization) bundle with pro-tech. This *is* the bright→dark
  green diagonal. Also moderately correlated with axis 3.
- **Why we set it aside as an axis:** intensity belongs on the **XP/engagement clock** (it's
  the thing gameplay grows over time). Making it a 4th *static* axis either double-counts the
  clock or forces us to give the clock up. Better used *as* the clock.
- **Visual channel:** energy / aura / charge — calm/contained ↔ charged/intense. (This is the
  clock's channel, layered over the 4 orientation channels.)

### Candidate C — Local ↔ Global *(scale / scope of moral concern)*
*At what scale do you locate the problem and the solution?* My watershed/community/bioregion ↔
the planet / global systems / international governance.

- **Source:** weaker / more fragmented — place attachment & bioregionalism vs cosmopolitan
  "planetary" environmentalism; the "think global, act local" tension. No single canonical
  instrument on par with NEP / EAI / cultural cognition.
- **Orthogonality to 1–3:** **MODERATE, with a flag** — risks correlating with *both* axis 3
  (local↔individual/community vs global↔collective/systemic) *and* the grid axis (grassroots
  skews local). It's squeezed between two axes we already have.
- **Visual channel:** scale / horizon / rootedness — rooted/place-bound (tree, soil) ↔
  migratory/planetary (ocean-current, migratory bird). Thematically gives the "Voyager /
  global systems thinker" flavor.

---

## "Is Local↔Global just the Technocratic↔Grassroots axis?" — No.

They **correlate** but **come apart** in important cases, because they answer different
questions:

- **Grid (A)** = *who holds power* — experts/centralized institutions vs distributed communities.
- **Scale (C)** = *where the action & concern are located* — here vs everywhere.

| | **Grassroots (bottom-up)** | **Technocratic (top-down)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Global scale** | Fridays for Future, Extinction Rebellion, transnational indigenous solidarity — *bottom-up AND planetary* | IPCC, carbon markets, climate treaties — *top-down AND planetary* |
| **Local scale** | community energy co-op, neighbourhood commons — *bottom-up AND local* | municipal smart-grid run by city engineers — *top-down AND local* |

All four cells are real and populated → the two axes are **distinct**. The correlation lives
on the diagonal (grassroots-local ↔ technocratic-global), but the off-diagonal cells
(global-grassroots, local-technocratic) are exactly the people the two axes disagree about.

## Also-considered, set aside

- **Nature fragile ↔ robust** (NEP limits / Cultural Theory benign↔ephemeral): heavily
  collinear with axis 2 (robust→tech-optimism, fragile→sufficiency) *and* overlaps the urgency
  clock. Redundant.
- **Hope ↔ Doom** (agency vs eco-anxiety/fatalism): a real *affective* construct (maps nicely
  to avatar mood), but it's an intensity/emotional layer, better expressed through the clock's
  aura channel than as a static identity axis.

---

## Recommendation & open question

**Lean: Candidate A (Technocratic ↔ Grassroots).** It's the only candidate that is *both*
empirically orthogonal to axes 1–3 *and* generates the archetype taxonomy (via group × grid).
Candidate B should power the **intensity clock**, not be a 4th axis. Candidate C is a
legitimate "Voyager flavor" axis but is the thinnest empirically and is squeezed between
axis 3 and the grid axis.

**Decision tensions to resolve:**
1. Do **Individual↔Collective (3)** and **Technocratic↔Grassroots (4)** feel distinct enough
   to a *user* answering a survey, or will they blur? (Most likely point of redundancy.)
2. If ecomania targets **non-Anglo markets early**, how much does the grid axis's weaker
   cross-cultural replication cost us — enough to prefer the thematically-rich-but-softer
   Local↔Global instead?
3. Do we want a 4th axis at all, or **3 orientation axes + a richer intensity clock**? (Three
   is the most defensible read of the literature.)
